Spicy Caesar Chicken Wrap


Ingredients
  • 1 lb of boneless skinless chicken thighs
  • 1 head of romaine lettuce
  • 3/4 cup of mayo
  • 4 gloves of garlic, grated
  • 1 tsp of worcestershire
  • 1 tsp of dijon mustard
  • 2 tsp of calibrian chili paste
  • 4 anchovy fillets, minced
  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1/4 cup of parmesean cheese
  • salt
  • pepper
  • olive oil
  • tortillas

Instructions
  1. Preheat air fryer to 400 degrees Fahrenheit.
  2. Pat the chicken dry and lay them flat. Drizzle olive oil over chicken and season however you like on both sides-- I use salt, pepper, smoked paprika powder, garlic powder, and Italian herbs.
  3. Throw chicken in the air fryer for about 10 minutes. Once finished cooking, let it rest before cutting it.
  4. For the dressing, grab a food proccessor or blender. Throw in your mayo, worcestershire, egg yolk, dijon mustard, anchovies, parmesean cheese, calabrian chili paste, a little olive oil, and salt and pepper to taste, then blend until smooth.
  5. Roughly chop Romaine lettuce and chicken and throw into salad bowl. Add a little extra parmesean cheese and croutons if you like. Sometimes I add some red onion, jalapeno, or tomatoes if I'm feeling frisky. Add the dressing and toss the salad.
  6. Lay your tortilla of choice down, add a big heaping scoop of salad to the center, then roll the tortilla into a wrap. Enjoy!
