Southwest Salad


Delicious southwest salad with a creamy cilantro lime dressing and a perfectly cooked ribeye on top


Ingredients
  • 1-2lb Ribeye steak
  • 5 oz Baby arugula
  • 1/2 Large onion
  • 1/2 Pint cherry tomatoes
  • 1-2 Avocados (cubed)
  • 1.5 Cups roasted corn
  • Large handful of cilantro
  • 3 Garlic cloves
  • 1 Jalepeno
  • 2 Limes
  • 3 Tbsp sour cream
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil
  • 1 Tbsp chile lime seasoning
  • 1/4 Cup of cotija cheese
  • Salt
  • Pepper
