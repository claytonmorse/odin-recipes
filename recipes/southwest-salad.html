Southwest Salad


Delicious southwest salad with a creamy cilantro lime dressing and a perfectly cooked ribeye on top


Ingredients
  • 1-2lb ribeye steak
  • 5 oz baby arugula
  • 1/2 large onion
  • 1/2 pint cherry tomatoes
  • 1-2 avocados (cubed)
  • 1.5 cups roasted corn
  • large handful of cilantro
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 jalepeno
  • 2 limes
  • 3 tbsp sour cream
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp chile lime seasoning
  • 1/4 Cup of cotija cheese
  • salt
  • pepper

Instructions
  1. Season ribeye with salt, pepper, any spices you like. Let rest for 1 hour to come up to room temp before cooking or grilling. I made mine medium rare in a cast iron pan and let rest while working through the rest of the recipe.
  2. For the salad, roughly chop 5oz of baby arugula. Add that to a salad bowl along with 1/2 a large onion (finely diced), half a pint of quartered cherry tomatoes, 1 to 2 avocados (cubed), and 1.5 cups of roasted corn.
  3. For the dressing, grab a small food processor or high powered blender, then process a large handful of cilantro, 3 garlic cloves, 1/2 jalapeno seed, 1/2 jalapeno with seeds, the juice of 1.5 limes, 3 tbsp of sour cream, 2 tbsp of olive oil, 1 tbsp of chile lime seasoning, and a generous pinch of salt. Give it a taste and adjust as you wish.
  4. Finally, cut the steak into thin slices, top the salad with the steak.
  5. Add 1/4 cup of cotija over the top.
  6. Pour over dressing, give it a toss, and enjoy!
